Grocery ERP 1.2.0

This version saves the tax of every purchase, lot and bill line, and makes purchases, lists and messages easier to use.

• Tax: every selling price and MRP includes tax. Each purchase line, lot and bill line keeps its tax name and one piece's Purchase price, Tax/unit and Purchase price incl. tax, so a later change to a tax rate does not alter them.
• Purchases: tick Bill rates: Include tax to type rates as the supplier's bill prints them. Remove a line with its bin button or Ctrl+Delete; totals update as you type. Void is for a purchase entered by mistake and needs its received date in an open year; send goods back with a vendor return.
• Billing: a name that matches several products lists them under the scan box. A held bill takes the date and number of the day it is paid.
• Screens: every table has a footer with the selection, row count and page arrows. A product, bill, purchase, vendor ledger or asset timeline opens in one panel beside the list or across the page, and each screen remembers which.
• Reports: seven remain: Profit and loss, Sales register, Item-wise sales, Purchase register, GST summary, Current unsold stock and Purchased vs used reconciliation. Low stock and expiry are Products filters, and balances due are in Vendors. The GST summary adds a cancelled vendor return back on the day it is cancelled.
• Dashboard: each tile opens exactly the list it counts. A lot is expiring within its product's or category's alert days (30 when neither sets them), and Stock value at cost includes archived products' stock.
• Returns: returned units of a deleted product go into a product you pick or create. Cancelling a vendor return whose lot was deleted puts the units in a new lot. Day end counts voided and edited cash expenses at their current amounts.
• Messages: a refused action now says why. When the weekly stock check finds lots that differ from their documents, a notice under the title bar offers Show differences.

Database schema: 39.
