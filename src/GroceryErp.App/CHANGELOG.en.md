Grocery ERP 1.2.0

This version saves the tax of every purchase, lot and bill line, adds help to every screen, and makes purchases, lists and messages easier to use.

• Help: every screen has an ⓘ beside its title. Click it, or press F1, to read what the screen is for and what each field, column and button does; F1 in a field opens the help at that field. The help follows the language you choose.
• Tax: every selling price and MRP includes tax. Each purchase line, lot and bill line keeps its tax name and one piece's Purchase price, Tax/unit and Purchase price incl. tax, so a later change to a tax rate does not alter them.
• Purchases: tick Bill rates: Include tax to type rates as the supplier's bill prints them. Remove a line with its bin button or Ctrl+Delete; totals update as you type, and a saved draft keeps its total. Void is for a purchase entered by mistake and needs its received date in an open year; it reverses the purchase's Expense lines on that date. Send goods back with a vendor return.
• Billing: as you type a name, from one Gujarati letter or two other characters, the matching products list under the scan box without Enter, each with the price of the batch a bill takes next, as the Products list shows it. A held bill takes the date and number of the day it is paid.
• Screens: every table has a footer with the selection, row count and page arrows, and a table's totals sit in a bar under it that wraps instead of cutting them off. A product, bill, purchase, vendor ledger or asset timeline opens in one panel beside the list or across the page, and each screen remembers which. Pages open from the sidebar at once, without a sliding animation.
• Reports: seven remain: Profit and loss, Sales register, Item-wise sales, Purchase register, GST summary, Current unsold stock and Purchased vs used reconciliation. Low stock and expiry are Products filters, and balances due are in Vendors. Pick a product by its name, SKU or barcode and a vendor from a list. The GST summary ends sales and purchases each with a subtotal instead of adding their tax together, and adds a cancelled vendor return back on the day it is cancelled.
• Dashboard: each tile opens exactly the list it counts. Payments today counts the cash kept after the change given back, so it adds up to Today's sales. A lot is expiring within its product's or category's alert days (30 when neither sets them), and Stock value at cost includes archived products' stock.
• Returns: returned units of a deleted product go into a product you pick or create. Cancelling a vendor return whose lot was deleted puts the units in a new lot. A return receipt's Gross plus Round off makes the refund. Day end counts voided and edited cash expenses at their current amounts.
• Money out: Record advance is gone. A month's salary run records the whole pay, including money handed over earlier in the month, and a payment after the run is an expense.
• Fixes: Sales history finds a bill by a product's SKU, and a bill's panel shows the change given back. The vendor panel's Purchases, Payments and Vendor returns tabs show the right balance due. Saved codes such as payment modes show as words. Setup no longer asks for an A4 printer.
• Messages: a refused action now says why. When the weekly stock check finds lots that differ from their documents, a notice under the title bar offers Show differences.

Database schema: 42.
