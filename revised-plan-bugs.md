A heap snapshot of that process (ClrMD, a scratch tool, nothing added to the repo) gave the path `TranslationSource` → `PropertyChanged` → `PagerBar` → page.
  - **Fixed 6 Nov 2026.** `PagerBar` removes its handler before adding it, as `LanguageRefresh` already did, so it holds at most one. `ActionStrip` and `FilterRow`, which use the same pattern and can sit in a panel (the product panel's Lots tab), do the same. No other change.

## Found by the calculation audit (7 November 2026)

The owner asked whether every figure (stats, reports, bills, product, sales, lot, asset and money-out entries) is calculated correctly. Every money-writing path was read, then `AuditScenarioTests` (new) entered an October of a shop through the services: a purchase with other charges, an Expense line and an Internal-use line, a purchase voided the next day, a vendor payment, a direct lot, bills with line and bill discounts and round off up and down, a void, a partial and a damaged return, two vendor returns (one cancelled), internal use, a damage and its correction, a count shortage and surplus, expenses edited and voided, an advance and a salary run, and an asset sold mid-month. About 90 figures were worked out by hand from the inputs and compared with the services, every report, the dashboard, day end, the vendor balance and the receipts, and 11 SQL checks compare each projection and document header with what its source rows add up to. A second test does the same in Composition mode and for a purchase typed with rates including tax.

Everything that is booked was right to the paisa: lot costs and tax, every bill and return, every P&L head, the sales and purchase registers, item-wise sales, GST, stock value, reconciliation, vendor balance, depreciation, disposal and day end. The 11 projection checks also passed on a generated 19,200-bill store (DataGen; its purchase headers do not match their lines by design, so that one check applies only to stores the services wrote). What was wrong is how five figures were shown or saved, below, and four accounting choices the owner should make.

- [x] **B-16 The dashboard's "Payments today" counted the change given back as cash** (Medium; reproduced by the scenario: 1,444 shown against 1,004 of sales)
  - `sale_payment` saves the cash handed over and the sale saves the change. Day end subtracts the change; the dashboard summed the payments, so its modes added up to more than today's sales whenever change was given.
  - **Fixed.** The dashboard's cash is what was kept: cash handed over less the change of today's bills that took cash, as day end counts it, so the modes add up to **Today's sales**. The dashboard help and both guides say so.
- [x] **B-17 A saved purchase draft showed a total of 0.000** (Medium; reproduced: the scenario's draft listed 0.000 instead of 2,571.000)
  - `SaveDraftAsync` priced the lines but never wrote the header's totals, so the Purchases list's **Total** and **Bill difference**, the bulk void list and the draft footer in the panel read the schema default.
  - **Fixed.** Saving a draft writes its subtotal, discount, tax, round off and total, from the same `Totals` helper posting uses (posting still works them out again at the rates in force then). A draft saved before this fix shows 0.000 until it is saved again; the production store starts empty.
- [x] **B-18 The GST summary added tax on sales to tax on purchases** (Medium; reproduced: one "Tax" total of 358.44 = 121.94 collected + 236.50 paid)
  - The report's totals summed every money column over both directions, on screen, in the Excel and A4 total row and in the CSV's last line; the sum means nothing to an accountant.
  - **Fixed.** Each direction ends with a **Subtotal** row (on screen, Excel and A4, and in the full CSV), there is no combined total row, and the footer shows **Taxable value of sales**, **Tax on sales**, **Taxable value of purchases** and **Tax on purchases** (`TabularReport.FooterTotals`; four strings, Gujarati marked MT). The Reports help and both guides say so.
- [x] **B-19 A return receipt's Gross plus Round off did not make the refund** (Low; reproduced: Gross 114.882 + Round off 0.081, Refund total 114.882)
  - The return receipt gave the refund, which already holds the bill's round-off share, as Gross, then printed the round off again. Any return from a bill rounded to the rupee showed it.
  - **Fixed.** Gross is the goods' value, taxable + tax, so Gross + Round off = Refund total. A return receipt saved earlier is corrected when it is read, so a reprint adds up too.
- [x] **B-20 The bill panel's footer showed cash handed over without the change** (Low; found in the uncommitted totals footer)
  - A bill of 560 paid with 1,000 cash read "Bill total ₹560 · Cash ₹1,000".
  - **Fixed.** `SaleDetails.Change` carries the change and the footer lists **Change** after the payments, as the receipt does. The Sales history help and both guides say so.
- [x] **O-9 A salary advance is counted again by the salary run** (owner decision, 7 November: remove the advance)
  - **Record advance** saved an `OTHER` salary payment, which counts as salary. The salary run then pre-fills the month's full salary and has nowhere to take the advance off, so unless the operator lowered the run by hand the employee was paid twice and P&L showed both: the scenario's 9,800 month showed 11,800. implementation.md lists advance recovery as backlog; the help did not warn.
  - Recommendation was an **Advance recovered** column in the salary run. The owner chose instead to remove the advance: the run records each month's whole pay, including money handed over earlier in the month, which its notes can mention.
  - **Done.** `AdvanceFormWindow`, the **Record advance** button, `MoneyOutService.RecordOtherSalaryPaymentAsync`, the strings `MoneyOut_Advance`, `MoneyOut_RecordAdvance` and `MoneyOut_AdvanceContext`, the `advance-form` help in both languages, its index row and its screenshot are gone (46 dialogs have help now). No migration: `kind='OTHER'` stays allowed, so an Other payment saved by an earlier version still shows, counts as salary and can be edited or voided. The Money out and salary run help and both guides say a payment after a month's run is an expense. Tests: the scenario's October salary is 9,800; `MoneyOutTests` keeps an earlier version's Other payment counting as salary (written as that version saved it) and corrects a salary run's payment instead of an advance.
- [x] **O-10 A count surplus never reaches P&L** (owner decision, 7 November: kept; no change)
  - A count shortage becomes a damage, an expense at cost. A surplus becomes a new lot at the counted lot's cost with no P&L entry, so goods found again after a shortage are expensed twice (the damage, then cost of goods when sold), and found goods that were never bought are charged at cost when sold.
  - **Kept.** When written-off goods turn up, correcting that damage (Money out → Damages → Edit or Void) puts the units back into their lot and reverses the loss, so they are booked once; doing so instead of counting them is the operator's responsibility. `OwnerDecisions_OnAFreshStore` shows the counted case.
- [x] **O-11 Internal-use purchases never reach P&L** (owner decision, 7 November: kept, as the plan decided)
  - An Internal-use line creates a lot outside P&L, and consuming it books nothing, so money spent on its goods shows in the vendor balance and the purchase register but in no expense. The plan says so (§1, "Product types"), and the purchase help calls Internal use "a lot outside profit and loss".
  - **Kept**, intentionally. `OwnerDecisions_OnAFreshStore` shows that its use books nothing.
- [x] **O-12 A voided purchase's Expense line is reversed on the void date** (owner decision, 7 November: as recommended)
  - Voiding a purchase takes it out of its own date's register and GST summary, but its Expense line's reversal was dated the void day, while every other correction is dated on the original date while that year is open. A purchase of 31 October voided on 2 November showed its freight in October and the reversal in November.
  - **Done.** `ApplyVoid` dates the reversal on the purchase's received date, for a single void and the bulk void alike, so a voided purchase leaves its own date's P&L as a voided bill does. Both refuse a purchase whose date is in a locked year (unchanged, tested in `StockInTests`), so the reversal never reaches a saved year. Nothing else reads the date: the weekly P&L check compares sales and returns only, the vendor ledger keeps the void on the day it was done, and the expense list, day end and dashboard do not date it. Past reversals keep their dates. The Purchases help and both guides say the void takes the Expense lines out of that date's P&L. Test: `OwnerDecisions_OnAFreshStore` voids a purchase of 25 October on 26 October; the 25th nets to nothing and the 26th has no entry.

Not changed, as documented: day end's **Cash expected** subtracts cash expenses but not cash salary, advances or vendor payments ("what the drawer should hold from the day's business"); a scale label's amount becomes a quantity at the lot's price, so a label printed at another price sells that amount, not that weight.

None of these comes from data made before a schema change. The scenario store is created by the migrations at schema 42 and written only through today's services. On commit 89d0d77, before these fixes, the same scenario in a separate worktree, again on a new store, showed exactly B-16 to B-20 (the bill panel listing 1,000 of payments on a 560 bill, with no change field to show) and nothing else. The owner-decision test, then named `OwnerDecisions_AsBuilt_OnAFreshStore`, reproduced O-10 to O-12 on a new store and passed unchanged there, so O-9 to O-12 were how the committed code behaved. The one inconsistency that did come from data was DataGen's: each generated purchase line had a fixed tax of 500 and line total of 10,500 whatever its quantity and rate, and each header 10,000 per line, so on a demo store (Start-Demo, ScaleAudit fixtures) a purchase's totals, the Purchase register, inward GST and vendor balances did not add up. The app was not at fault: every purchase the services wrote passed the same check, and lots, sales and P&L in DataGen stores are consistent.
  - **DataGen fixed (owner, 7 November).** A line's tax is 5% of quantity × rate, its total their sum, and the header's totals are its lines' sums, set as the purchase is posted, so vendor balances follow. Generation now refuses a store with a purchase that does not add up. A store generated at scale 0.02 (12,800 bills) passed all 11 projection checks, the purchase one included. Stores generated earlier, such as the fixtures in `artifacts`, keep the old amounts until regenerated; nothing in the tests or ScaleAudit depended on them.

These figures were wrong in release 1.2.0 too, except B-20. The fixes are on `screen-help` for the release after 1.2.0; putting them in 1.2.0 means rebuilding the packages and rerunning G-3 and G-4.
- **Decided 8 Nov 2026:** the owner chose to put them in 1.2.0, with the rest of `screen-help` (the screen help and the totals footer), rather than release the 6 November packages with figures known to be wrong; no human gate had run on those. The 1.2.0 packages are rebuilt from `screen-help` and their gates rerun (G-3, G-4 and G-5).
