# Consume for internal use

Record stock of an internal-use product used in the shop, such as carry bags or cleaning supplies.

The quantity leaves the lot. Internal-use stock is kept out of billing and of profit and loss.

## Fields

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Quantity** | How much was used, up to the lot's stock. |
| **Reason** | What it was used for. |

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Takes the quantity out of the lot. |
| **Cancel** | Closes without a change. |
