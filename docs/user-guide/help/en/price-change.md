# Change price

Sell the selected line at another price on this bill only.

The lot keeps its own price for other bills. Changing the line's quantity or lot afterwards takes the lot's price again.

## Fields

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Price** | The price of one unit, tax included. It must be above zero and cannot be above the line's MRP. |

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Changes the price. Enter does the same. |
| **Cancel** | Closes without a change. Esc does the same. |
