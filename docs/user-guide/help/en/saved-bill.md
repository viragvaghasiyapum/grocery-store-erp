# Saved bill

Carry on with the bill that was left unfinished, or discard it and start a new one.

Billing keeps one unfinished bill. When you leave Billing, or close the app, before a bill is paid, the bill is saved, and the next time Billing opens it asks this. The line under the question says when the bill was started, how many lines it has, what it comes to and, when it has one, its customer. The question has no close button: choose one of the two.

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Continue bill** | Opens the bill to carry on with it. Enter does the same. |
| **Discard bill** | Deletes the bill and its lines. It has taken no stock, number or payment, so nothing else changes. |
