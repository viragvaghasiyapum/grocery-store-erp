# Set category, tax rate or product type

Pick the one category, tax rate or product type to give every selected product.

The title names the action, and the line under it says which products are selected. Nothing changes in this window: **Continue** opens the review, which shows how many products will change and which are skipped, and nothing is saved until you apply it there.

## Fields

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Shown for **Set category…**. The category every selected product moves to; only active categories are listed. A product already in it is skipped. |
| **Tax rate** | Shown for **Set tax rate…**. The tax rate the selected products are sold and bought at from now on; only active rates are listed. Saved lots and bills keep the rate they had. A product already at this rate is skipped. |
| **Product type** | Shown for **Set product type…**. **Saleable** (sold on bills) or **Internal use** (used in the shop and kept out of billing and profit and loss). A product with stock keeps its type and is skipped, as is one that already has this type. |

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Continue** | Opens the review of the bulk action for the selected products. With nothing chosen, it asks you to choose a value. |
| **Cancel** | Closes the window; nothing changes. |
